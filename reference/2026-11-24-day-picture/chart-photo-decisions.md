## KOS 사진 흐름 (HAR 에서 읽은 모양 — 값 아님)

모두 내부 API(`api.kos-solution.com/tenants/{tenant}/…`) · POST · 브라우저 User-Agent 필요.

1. `meta/codes/v1/queries/get-codes {category: "IMAGE_CATEGORY"}` · `{category: "IMAGE_EQUIPMENT"}` → `data[] {id, category, name, order, closed, default}`
2. `image/v1/commands/generate-presigned-url {fileName}` → `data.url`(S3 presigned PUT). 파일 이름 모양 = `{고객 이름}_{YYYYMMDD}_{uuid}.png`
3. `PUT <presigned url>` · `Content-Type: image/png` · 인증 헤더 없음
4. `image/visitor-image/v1/commands/register-visitor-images {registerVisitorImageConfigurations: [{fileName, visitorId, source: "HOSPITAL", equipment: {코드 객체 통째}, scheduleId, shootingDateTimeUtc, memo, category: {코드 객체 통째}}]}` → `{}` · 여러 장이면 배열에 여러 개(`사진등록다수.har` — presigned 3 → 등록 1회)
5. `image/visitor-image/v1/queries/get-visitor-images {visitorId, scheduleId, deleted: false}` → `data[] {id, fileName, category, equipment, memo, shootingDateTimeUtc, …}`
6. `image/v1/commands/generate-signed-urls-with-param {fileNames[], query: {w, h, f?, q?}}` → 썸네일(108×108 · webp) · `query` 없으면 원본
7. 삭제 `image/visitor-image/v1/commands/delete-visitor-images {ids: [...]}`
